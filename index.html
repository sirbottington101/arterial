How to play

  • Drag to draw roads. Join each house to a hub of the same colour.
  • Cars drive themselves. Each dot in a hub is a car it is waiting for; every arrival is a journey.
  • If a hub fills up, a red ring starts. Clear its traffic before the ring closes.
  • Every road cell uses a tile. Erase (or right-drag) gives tiles back.
  • Drag straight across water or rock to build a bridge or tunnel.
  • Motorways link two roads directly and fast. Roundabouts and lights let more cars through a busy junction.
  • Each week the city limits grow and you pick an upgrade.
  • Pinch or scroll to zoom. You can build while paused.
